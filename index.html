How to Use PO Signal
Step-by-Step Guide
1
Open Pocket Option & Switch to OTC Market
Login to your Pocket Option account. In the asset selector, scroll down and choose any OTC pair (e.g. EUR/USD OTC). OTC markets are available 24/7, including weekends.
2
Find a Matching Signal on This Dashboard
Look for the same pair shown on your broker screen. Make sure the signal is not expired — the countdown ring must still be running (green or yellow).
3
Check Signal Direction & Confidence
The signal shows ▲ CALL (price goes UP) or ▼ PUT (price goes DOWN). Only trade signals with confidence ≥ 75% for better accuracy.
