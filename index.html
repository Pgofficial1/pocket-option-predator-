How to Use PO Signal
Step-by-Step Guide
1
Open Pocket Option & Switch to OTC
Login and choose any OTC pair (e.g. EUR/USD OTC) from the asset selector. OTC is available 24/7 including weekends.
2
Find the Matching Signal
Find the same pair on this dashboard. Make sure the countdown ring is still running — green or yellow means safe to trade.
3
Check Direction & Confidence
▲ CALL = price goes UP  |  ▼ PUT = price goes DOWN. Only trade signals with confidence ≥ 75%.
4
Set Expiry on Pocket Option
Match the Expiry shown (e.g. "2 min") on your broker. Enter the trade immediately — do not wait.
5
Place Trade & Wait
Click BUY or SELL and wait. Never trade an EXPIRED signal — when the card shows EXPIRED, skip it completely.
